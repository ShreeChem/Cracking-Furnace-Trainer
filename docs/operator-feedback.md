# Operator feedback guide

Thank you for testing the cracking furnace trainer. It takes about 15 minutes. Please play at least one scenario in **Trainee** mode first, then read its debrief.

## Who you are (optional)

- Role: board operator, field operator, shift leader, OTS/simulation engineer, instructor, other
- Years in ethylene or other fired-heater units
- Language you used: DE / EN / FR

## Questions

1. **Realism of the response.** When you acted (cut feed, changed COT, opened the damper), did the furnace respond in the direction and roughly at the speed you expect? What felt wrong?
2. **Alarms and trips.** Were the alarm texts, priorities and trip limits believable? Which limit or delay would you change, and to what?
3. **The expected steps.** In the debrief, is the expected procedure what your unit would do? Is anything missing, in the wrong order, or plant-specific?
4. **Scenario 03 (rich fuel gas).** The trainer expects *cut fuel first, then restore air slowly*. Does that match your training?
5. **Scenario 06 (drifting thermocouple).** Would you deselect the bad thermocouple from the average, or put the COT controller in manual? Is that possible on your DCS?
6. **Trip response.** After a trip, the trainer keeps dilution steam on the coils (partial trip) or cuts all fuel (combustion trip). Does that match your furnaces?
7. **Screen.** Is the overview bar useful? Which value would you add for a cracking furnace?
8. **Language.** If you used German or French, which terms sound wrong or unusual in your plant?
9. **Use.** Would you use something like this for refresher training, shift handover practice, or onboarding new operators?
10. **One change.** If you could change one thing, what would it be?

## How to send feedback

- On GitHub: open an issue with the **Operator feedback** template, or
- Reply to the LinkedIn post or send a direct message.

Please do not share plant names, tag lists, limits or any data from your employer. General experience is enough.
