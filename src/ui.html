SHIFT

Act in timeor the furnace acts for you

Scenarios 01 to 05 end in a trip if you wait too long. Scenario 06 has no trip: it tests whether you believe a drifting instrument. The surprise shift picks one at random and hides its name.

The procedure and the scoring rules for each scenario open after your first try, so the first attempt stays a real test.

Four stepsBriefing to debrief

01Briefing

Plant state and limits are given up front. In trainee mode the procedure is not: like on a real shift, you have to know it.

02Live furnace

An upset starts. Values, trends and alarms move every second. You change setpoints, switch modes, start equipment and call people.

03Automatic evaluation

Every action is time-stamped and checked against the procedure: the right step, in time, within limits, without harmful moves.

04Explained debrief

A score in four parts, your actions next to the expected steps, and a short note on what to do differently.

04

The furnaceanswers for you

No actionThe upset keeps developing. Alarms escalate from medium to high priority.
Limit crossedThe interlock acts on its own: a partial trip (feed cut, firing reduced, steam kept on the coils) or a full fuel trip for combustion hazards.
Scenario ends"Furnace tripped." Missed steps score zero and the total is capped at 40.
Wrong actionA harmful move, such as adding air to a fuel-rich firebox, costs 5 points and is explained in the debrief.
Bad instrumentNothing trips, but acting on a false reading cuts production for no reason. Cross-check before you act.
